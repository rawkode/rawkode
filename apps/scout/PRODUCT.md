# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Keyboard-fluent Mac users who spend much of their day navigating projects, documents, and mounted storage. They need a familiar spatial model, fast command-driven navigation, and trustworthy file operations without granting an app more access than necessary.

## Product Purpose

Scout is a sandboxed macOS file manager that combines Finder-familiar locations and file semantics with persistent Miller columns and a focused command palette. Success means users can grant only the locations they choose, move through large hierarchies without losing context, inspect files quickly, and complete common operations entirely from the keyboard.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, fast. Scout should feel native and dependable during sustained daily use. Speed is expressed through response time, compact hierarchy, and keyboard fluency rather than visual noise.

## Anti-references

Do not become a dual-pane commander, a terminal wrapper, or a dashboard. Avoid novelty controls, decorative glass, oversized cards, dense unstructured metadata, and unrestricted filesystem assumptions. Do not imitate Finder defects merely for familiarity.

## Design Principles

- Preserve place: every selection should make its parent hierarchy obvious.
- Earn trust: access, conflicts, errors, and destructive operations must be explicit and recoverable.
- Make the fast path visible: menus, toolbars, and keyboard commands describe the same actions.
- Stay native: use macOS conventions and system behavior unless Scout has a concrete reason to differ.
- Keep private work local: no analytics, network service, or custom content index.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet a full native macOS accessibility baseline: VoiceOver, Full Keyboard Access, increased contrast, reduced motion, color-independent state, clear focus, and localization-ready user-facing strings.
