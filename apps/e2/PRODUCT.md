# Apsides

## Register

product

## Users

A single signed-in owner uses Apsides throughout the day to write portable
notes, connect trusted services, and turn people, events, work, and knowledge
into a typed personal workspace. The primary task is writing and following
context without leaving the document flow.

## Product Purpose

Apsides is a calm, strongly typed knowledge layer centered on Today and
Supertags. Notes remain authoritative documents. Canonical entities connect
those documents to user-defined structure and attributable Google and GitHub
observations without making provider records the product model.

Success means the owner can capture text, link or create an entity in place,
follow it through a stable pane trail, and trust that synchronized data never
silently overwrites authored knowledge.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and command-center-like. The interface should feel native,
focused, and dependable during sustained use.

## Anti-references

Do not build a generic SaaS dashboard, a feature wall, an unrelated card
collection, or a decorative AI-workflow interface. Do not give integrations,
administration, and secondary tools the same visual weight as the writing
surface. Do not copy Cloudflare branding or component vocabulary.

## Design Principles

- Keep Today, documents, and Supertags at the center of the product hierarchy.
- Let the writing canvas dominate; reveal structure and provenance in context.
- Preserve stable identity across documents, integrations, renames, and merges.
- Make automated writes attributable, reversible where possible, and honest
  about partial synchronization.
- Prefer familiar product controls and progressive disclosure over modal flows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, visible focus,
semantic labels and status announcements, readable contrast, zoom and narrow
viewports, reduced motion, and interaction states that do not rely on color
alone.
