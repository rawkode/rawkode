# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People writing and revisiting personal daily notes, now in the Enchiridion website. Keyboard control, responsiveness, and low idle resource use matter.

## Product Purpose

Open directly into today's Loro-backed document. Rich text, lists, tables, and diagrams share a continuous editor. Configurable presentation slots bring calendar events, people, and other daily context alongside writing, with defaults until customization is available.

## Brand Personality

Native, focused, responsive. Carry forward the notebook direction from PR #33.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like chrome, fixed formatting bars, permanent date selectors, and controls competing with writing.

## Design Principles

- Writing is the primary surface.
- Commands are discoverable and keyboard accessible.
- Formatting appears in context.
- Navigation preserves saved and unsaved state.
- External context never overwrites the document.
- Verify real interactions, not only builds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain native selection, labeled controls, keyboard navigation, system appearance, and reduced-motion support.
