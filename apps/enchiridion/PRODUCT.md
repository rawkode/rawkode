# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People writing and revisiting personal daily notes on Mac and iPhone. The current
user prioritizes keyboard control, responsiveness, and low idle resource use.

## Product Purpose

Make it immediate to write in today's document and revisit another day. A day
becomes a persisted Loro document only on first content edit. Rich text, lists, tables, and inline diagrams share a continuous Tiptap editor.

## Brand Personality

Native, focused, responsive. The requested quality standard is a polished Apple
app. Specific visual references are optional and remain open for refinement.

## Anti-references

The user explicitly rejected the persistent date selector and fixed formatting
bar. Avoid dashboard-like chrome, permanent tool rows, and controls competing
with writing. Avoid idle CPU work and unbounded document retention.

## Design Principles

- Writing is the primary surface.
- Commands are discoverable and keyboard accessible.
- Formatting appears in the context of a selection.
- Navigation preserves saved and unsaved document state.
- Verify real interaction and resource use, not only builds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain native text selection, VoiceOver labels, keyboard navigation, system
appearance, and reduced-motion support. No additional user-specific needs have
been stated.
