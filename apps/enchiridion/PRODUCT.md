# Enchiridion Product

## Register

product

## Users

Enchiridion is a private knowledge workspace for one person moving between a Mac, iPhone, calendar, and CarPlay. The primary job is to capture and connect daily thinking without translating it into database syntax or maintaining a separate filing system.

## Product Purpose

Enchiridion makes the daily note the stable center of work. Calendar events, related pages, structured objects, saved views, and whiteboards remain close at hand, but they support writing instead of replacing it. Local storage is authoritative, iCloud transports changes between devices, and calendar access stays read-only.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and native. The interface should feel like a focused Apple-platform writing tool: immediately understandable, quietly capable, and trustworthy with personal information.

## Anti-references

- Calendar dashboards that make an agenda the main content of Today.
- Visible database or query syntax inside ordinary prose.
- Card-heavy productivity dashboards, gamified coaching, and decorative glass.
- Touch interactions copied onto macOS without sidebars, keyboard access, or pointer affordances.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the daily note central and reveal supporting context around it.
2. Preserve readable prose while making linked people, events, pages, and types inspectable.
3. Prefer familiar platform behavior over custom chrome.
4. Make local durability and sync state explicit without interrupting writing.
5. Reveal complexity progressively: writing first, structure when requested.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, keyboard access, system contrast, and reduced-motion-aware transitions. Gestures must have visible or assistive alternatives, and meaning must never depend on color alone.
